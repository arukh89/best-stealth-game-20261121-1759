Pale Lantern — The Herbarium Heist: a top-down stealth game. Wren, a seed-thief with a shuttered lantern, slips through the night herbarium to steal the Moonpetal Seed. Three levels (Fern Greenhouse, Pressed-Flower Archive, Moonpetal Vault), raycast vision cones, fern cover, lamplight danger zones, suspicion that decays, pebble lures and noise rings. Collect the pressed-flower seals, reach the green exit door unseen.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Move with WASD or arrow keys, hold Shift to sneak, click to throw a pebble, M to mute.
